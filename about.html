Our Story

In-House Catering Solutions was founded in 2010 with a straightforward mission: to transform the way people experience institutional and event catering. We believed that schools, corporate venues, and large gatherings deserved more than reheated, uninspired meals. They deserved restaurant quality food, prepared with care and served with pride.

What began as a small operation run by a dedicated team has grown into one of the most trusted catering partners in the region. Our passion for quality drives everything we do, from sourcing ingredients locally wherever possible to building menus that genuinely excite and nourish.

Over the years we have built lasting relationships with suppliers, schools, and event organisers who share our commitment to excellence. Our approach combines operational efficiency with a genuine love of great food, ensuring every service we deliver meets the highest standards.
